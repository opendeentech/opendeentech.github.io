AyatShuffle Privacy Policy

Developer: Open Deen Tech (OpenDeenTech)
Effective date: November 21, 2026
Android application ID: org.opendeen.ayatshuffle

AyatShuffle helps you track memorized Quran verses and choose verses for revision. Your memorization selections are stored on your device. The app does not send those selections to Open Deen Tech and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information handled by the app

AyatShuffle stores the suras and verses you mark as memorized in the app's local storage. It uses those selections to calculate progress and generate shuffled revision recommendations. Quran text and metadata are bundled with the app.

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or advertising identifier. Open Deen Tech does not collect your memorization history through the app.

Sharing, advertising, and tracking

The app does not sell or transmit your memorization selections to advertisers, analytics providers, or developer-operated servers. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. That information is used to respond to your request. Email providers process those communications under their own terms and privacy policies. Please do not send passwords or information that is unnecessary for your request.

Android backup and Google Play

Android backup is enabled for this app. Depending on your device and account settings, Android may back up app data to your configured backup provider or transfer it to another device. Open Deen Tech does not operate that backup service or receive your backup. You can manage backups in your device and account settings.

Google Play and Android may separately process installation, diagnostic, and other platform information under their own policies and your settings. See Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's services.

Retention and deletion

Memorization selections remain in local app storage until you change or remove them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. To remove saved selections within the app, open each selected sura and choose Unselect All. To erase all local app data at once, use Android Settings, open Apps, select AyatShuffle, and clear its storage. Menu names may vary by device.

Clearing local storage or uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove previously created system backups. Manage or delete those backups through your backup provider. Android may restore saved data when the app is installed again.

Support emails may remain in our mailbox while we handle your request and retain necessary support records. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the contact address below; provider backups and legal retention requirements may affect deletion.

Security

Memorization data is stored in the app's private local storage, protected by Android's application isolation and your device security. The app does not add its own encryption to this data. No device, storage system, or email service can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's privacy

The app does not require children or other users to submit personal information to Open Deen Tech. A parent or guardian who has questions about information sent in a support email can contact us below.

This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages and contains no app-specific tracking scripts. GitHub may process visitor information, such as IP addresses, when serving the page. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data handling changes. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

For privacy questions or support-data deletion requests, contact Open Deen Tech at opendeentech@gmail.com.
